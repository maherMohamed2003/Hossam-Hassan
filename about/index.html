About

Hossam Hassan

Interior finishing engineer

Twelve years of turning shells into finished spaces.

Eng. Hossam Hassan has spent more than 12 years on interior finishing sites across Cairo and Giza — running the material selection, execution and day-to-day supervision that take a space from raw concrete to a place people actually live and work in.

The work spans apartments, villas, duplexes, offices, shops, restaurants, hospitals and clinics. The scale changes; the standard doesn't — materials are chosen for how they age, not just how they photograph, and every detail is checked on site rather than assumed from a drawing.

Recent work includes a 270 m² twin house inside Compound Royal Meadows, Sheikh Zayed — full finishing from shell to handover, built predominantly with Egyptian materials.

How the work is approached

Three things that don't change.

Exploit the space

Layout and finishing decisions are made together, so every square meter is used with intent — not filled in after the fact.

Honest materials

A preference for high-grade Egyptian marble, wood and stone, with imported materials used only where they add something the local market can't.

Presence on site

Supervision happens in person, day to day, so issues are caught while they're still cheap and easy to fix.
