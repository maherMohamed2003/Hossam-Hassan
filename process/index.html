From shell to handover

Process

Every project follows the same sequence, so nothing is decided twice and nothing is left for later.

01

Site visit & briefing

A walkthrough of the space as handed over, plus a conversation about how it will be used, the budget and the timeline. This sets the scope before anything is priced.

02

Design & material selection

Layout, finishes and fixtures are chosen together — flooring, wall treatments, ceiling design, marble, wood and lighting — favoring Egyptian materials with imports used selectively.

03

Quotation & schedule

A clear scope of work, pricing and a realistic timeline, agreed before any site work begins.

04

Site preparation

Protection, chasing for electrical and plumbing, and coordination with any other trades already on site.

05

Core execution

Flooring, ceilings, partitions, painting and cladding, carried out in sequence under daily supervision from the engineer.

06

Joinery & fixtures

Custom woodwork, doors, kitchens and bathroom fittings installed once the core finishing is stable and dust-free.

07

Detailing & snagging

A full pass through the space to correct any detail that isn't right, before the client sees the finished work.

08

Client walkthrough & handover

A joint walkthrough with the client, any final adjustments, and formal handover of the completed space.
